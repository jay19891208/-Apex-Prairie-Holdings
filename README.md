## What we do

**Apex Prairie Quality Systems**: on-site quality walkthroughs for contractors, property managers and small manufacturers.
- Up to 90-minute site walkthrough
- Objective photos organized by finding
- Priority-ranked corrective-action log with owner, target date and status
- Client-ready PDF report delivered within 24 hours

**Apex Quality Hub**: licensed operations software for companies.
- Company, sites, users and access per site
- Process designer with stages, dependencies and inspection criteria
- Projects and work orders with timed execution
- Independent inspection and approval, holds, rework and full audit trail
- Time tracking, inventory by location and lot
- Offline-signed licensing, backup and restore
